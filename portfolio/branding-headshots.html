    Questions

    Frequently asked

    Don't see your question? Ask Michael directly.

    What's the difference between brand photography and a headshot?

    A headshot is a single, polished portrait, usually for a profile, directory or press kit. Brand photography is a broader library: you at work, your space, your process and details, so your website, deck and social feed all feel like one brand.

    Can you photograph our whole team on-site?

    Yes. Team sessions can be set up at your office with matched lighting and framing, so every headshot in your directory looks like it belongs together.

    How should I prepare for a brand session?

    Before the shoot Michael talks through your business, audience and where the images will be used, then builds a shot list with you. Bring outfit options in solid colors and any props or products that tell your story.

    How long until I get my photos?

    Edited images are delivered in an online gallery within 1 to 2 weeks of your session.

    Do you travel outside San Antonio?

    Yes. The studio is based in San Antonio and regularly works with clients in Austin, Houston and Dallas.
