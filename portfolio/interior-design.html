    Questions

    Frequently asked

    Don't see your question? Ask Michael directly.

    Who do you photograph interiors for?

    Interior designers, builders, architects, restaurants, hotels and other hospitality spaces that need images for portfolios, publications, awards and marketing.

    Do you style the space for the shoot?

    Each room is styled and composed before shooting. Cerissa McKinnis, an interior design author and the studio's creative advisor, brings a designer's eye to the details.

    Can the images be used for publication and awards?

    Yes. Usage is agreed up front.

    How long does an interiors shoot take?

    It depends on the number of rooms and the light. Share the project and Michael will plan the schedule around the best natural light.

    Do you shoot projects outside San Antonio?

    Yes. The studio regularly works in Austin, Houston and Dallas.
